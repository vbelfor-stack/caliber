### The approval channel is a new surface, deliberately

`stage_flip_approvals(ticker, from_stage, to_stage, rationale, approved_at)`. **NOT
`lifecycle_overrides`** — an override says *the approved stage REPLACES the computed one*

`beta`, plus the non-currency counts `shares_outstanding` and `analyst_count`.

**"ALL score-bearing fields" is read as "every score-bearing field a currency error can
REACH."** A ratio sharing a basis top and bottom reads identically in KRW and USD. Verified
on the live case rather than assumed: SKHY's `freeCashFlowYieldTTM` is **0.0777**, and KRW
TTM FCF ÷ KRW market cap reproduces **7.77%** — FMP's key-metrics block is internally
consistent on the issuer basis. Blocking those would have discarded valid data and
manufactured the absences fail-closed exists to prevent.

**Blast radius: exactly one name.** SKHY is the universe's only non-USD reporter; all 28 are
USD-quoted. The guard is universal in scope and binds on one issuer today.

**"Block" = `missing_prov` with a typed source**, e.g.
`fmp:currency:non_usd_blocked:KRW (reporting basis; ruled USD-only 2026-08-28, never
converted)`. Nothing is rescaled; there is no rate, no forex call and no arithmetic. Pinned
behaviourally *and* over the AST.

**UNKNOWN BLOCKS.** A field whose basis could not be established is withheld — including the
case where two statements disagree about the issuer's currency, which yields UNKNOWN rather
than a majority vote.
